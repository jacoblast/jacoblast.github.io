If you're searching for what piano tuning costs in the Portland area, here's a straight answer before the details: I charge **$175 for a basic tune, $250 for full service, and $450 and up for deep service**. No trip charge within about 25 miles of Portland.

That's the short version. But "how much does it cost" and "what do I actually need" are two different questions, and the second one matters more. Here's how I think about pricing, and the handful of things that move the number.

## The three tiers

**Basic Tune — $175.** A straightforward tuning visit for a piano that's already in good shape and on a regular schedule. If your piano was tuned in the last year or so and plays well, this is usually all it needs.

**Full Service — $250.** Tuning, plus time spent on whatever else the piano needs — touch, tone, lubrication, minor repairs. This is the visit I recommend for most pianos, because tuning alone doesn't address the mechanical and tonal drift that every piano experiences over time. It's the best way to keep an instrument improving visit after visit rather than just holding steady.

**Deep Dive — $450+.** An extended appointment for pianos that need more than a standard visit allows: significant regulation work for touch, a voicing overhaul for tone, or more involved repairs. This is priced by the scope of work, which is why it starts at $450 rather than landing on a single number.

## What actually affects the price

Most of the variation between visits comes down to three things.

**How long it's been since the last tuning.** This is the big one. A piano that hasn't been tuned in several years has usually dropped well below standard pitch, and bringing it back requires a *pitch raise* — essentially tuning the piano more than once in a single visit so it can hold the correction. I include pitch correction in the Full Service price, but it does draw from the time available for other improvements, and a severe case can be the difference between a routine visit and a deep one.

**The condition of the instrument.** A well-kept piano in a stable room tunes predictably. One that's been through years of humidity swings, a move, or long neglect takes more time and sometimes minor repair before it will hold a tune at all.

**How much beyond tuning you want done.** Touch and tone are separate from pitch. If a piano plays unevenly or sounds harsh, that's regulation and voicing work — and how far you want to take it is what separates a full service from a deep service.

## What's included at every visit

Whatever tier you book, a few things are always true: I come to you, I take the time to do the work properly (most visits run 1½ to 2 hours), and every visit is backed by a **30-day satisfaction guarantee** — if you're not happy with any aspect of it, I'll return at no charge.

## A note on location

Prices above apply throughout my primary service area — roughly 25 miles of Portland, which covers most of the metro and Vancouver, WA. For appointments beyond that, a modest travel supplement applies. You can see the [full service area here](/service-area/).

## The honest answer

Piano tuning in Portland generally runs somewhere between $150 and $300 for a standard visit, depending on the technician and what's included. My pricing sits squarely in that range, with the difference being how much I try to accomplish beyond pitch on a given visit.

If you're not sure which tier your piano needs, that's an easy conversation — tell me a bit about the instrument and when it was last serviced, and I'll give you a straight recommendation.
