Piano Repair in Portland, OR & Vancouver, WA

Sticking keys, broken strings, a buzzing note, a pedal that doesn't feel right — I fix all of it. Whether you're searching for a piano tuner or an old-fashioned piano repairman, you've landed in the right place. Most repairs happen on the spot, during a regular visit.

I don't upsell repairs you don't need. Most minor issues — a sticking key, a buzzing string, a loose part — are handled right there during your visit. Anything bigger gets an honest, upfront quote before I touch it.

What I fix.

  • Sticking or sluggish keys — often a quick on-the-spot fix, and a common sign of humidity changes or general wear.
  • Broken or buzzing strings — replaced or repaired, then tuned to match the rest of the piano.
  • Worn or broken hammers — addressed as part of regulation and voicing, or replaced if needed.
  • Malfunctioning pedals and dampers — sustain, soft, and sostenuto pedals all checked and adjusted.
  • Worn or broken action parts — the mechanism between key and hammer, repaired or replaced.
  • Evaluation and diagnosis — if something just feels or sounds off and you're not sure why.

Whatever's going on with your piano — old or new, a simple fix or something more involved — I'm happy to take a look and set it right.
