• Tuning — I work with sophisticated aural/analog techniques as well as the latest digital technology.
  • Pitch raises — re-tensioning the strings before tuning if the piano hasn't been tuned in a long time.
  • Regulation — calibrating the action mechanism to restore proper touch and response.
  • Voicing — manipulating the felt of the hammers to even out response and/or alter the tonal character of the instrument.
  • Lubrication — an essential (and often overlooked!) part of getting a piano to play at its best.
  • Repair — sticking keys, broken strings, broken hammers, malfunctioning pedals and dampers, and worn or broken action parts.
  • Evaluation and diagnosis — if something just feels or sounds off and you're not sure why.

Whatever kind of piano you have — old or new, a family heirloom or something you just picked up — I'm happy to schedule a visit and do everything I can to make it sing again.
