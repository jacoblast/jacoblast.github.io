Upright Piano Tuning & Repair in Portland, OR & Vancouver, WA

Whether it hasn't been touched in ten years, just moved across town, or the keys are starting to stick, I'd love to get your upright playing the way it should.

Upright pianos come in a wide range of sizes, from waist-high spinets, consoles, and studios, all the way to 60" tall upright grands. I have a special fondness for the unique character of old uprights, and with some TLC, the transformation in tone and touch can be amazing.
